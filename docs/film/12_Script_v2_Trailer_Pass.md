## DELIVERY NOTES — changed lines only

- **L2** — "Good." lands *with the half-smile*, a beat before the rest. He wanted to be asked.
- **L3** — "broke" is placed like an evidence bag set on a table. Not louder. Heavier.
- **L4** — first sentence fast; *"Anyone can say 'wrong'"* almost thrown away — confidence, not contempt.
- **L6** — three fragments, three jabs, counted like exhibits. *"Who noticed?"* is quiet. It's the first line that is genuinely hard to sit across from.
- **L7** — the anaphora softens ("And who…") as prosecution becomes advocacy. Warmth enters on *"plan."*
- **L8** — *"the student"* is the first time he says the word gently. The room should feel it.
- **L9** — micro-pause after *"with them."* That pause is the film's promise.
