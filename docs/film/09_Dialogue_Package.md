# ACT IV — THE MERGE

*Two lines across fifteen seconds. That ratio is the point.*

### L18 · JUDGE ZERO · Shot 16 · 2.22s @ 135 wpm
> **"Students don't need another chatbot."**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Emotional direction** | Something sacred is ending so something better can start. Not addressed to anyone. To everyone. |
| **Facial expression** | `CALM AUTHORITY` — unhurried, no valediction in it. |
| **Eye direction** | Middle distance, down the steps. **Not** to the Lawyer, **not** to camera. |
| **Camera** | Descent crane · 35mm · descending **with** him · full shot, low |
| **Notes** | **The one broken rule of the film — he leaves the bench.** Cane, weight, foot — never foot first. Breath visible (second and last time). Representatives fade out during this shot, **without defeat**. Gavel and glasses stay on the bench. |

### L19 · LAWYER ZERO · Shot 17 · 1.78s @ 135 wpm — ⚑ THE FILM LIVES HERE
> **"They need a mentor."**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Emotional direction** | Recognition. Close, warm, almost private — the student finishing the teacher's sentence. **It must sound like it costs him nothing to hand the film back to the old man.** Not triumphant. Not a punchline. |
| **Facial expression** | `SINCERE`. Then — **after** the line — both characters smile **at the same moment**. |
| **Eye direction** | To Judge Zero, held. Pure profile two-shot. |
| **Camera** | Merge two-shot · 50mm · eye level · slow 90° dolly to pure profile · shallow depth, room gone to black beyond two shafts of light |
| **Notes** | Deliberately slower than his other lines — he is not selling any more, which is why he sits at 135 wpm rather than 165. **The performance: they stop → a beat of nothing → both smile together.** The Judge's smile exists **exactly once in the entire film** and it should look like it has been waiting there for years. This beat gets its own animation pass and its own review. **Escalation: `motion_control` from a filmed human performance. If the smile does not land, manual animation. Do not ship a mechanical smile.** |
