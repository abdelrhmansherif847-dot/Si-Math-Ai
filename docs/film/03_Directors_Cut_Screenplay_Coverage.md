# PART 2 — COVERAGE: EVERY LINE OWNS A SHOT

Camera grammar for the film: **Judge Zero is always framed from below** (authority), **Lawyer Zero at eye level** (our advocate), **the Representatives from slightly above** (subjects of the question). Lenses: 24mm for the world, 35mm for movement, 50–85mm for faces. The camera only moves when a character or an idea moves — every move below names its reason.

## ACT I — THE SESSION

### SHOT 1 — The Strike *(no line)*
- **Lens/height/size/move:** 85mm macro · bench height · extreme CU on the gavel · locked.
- **Speaking/listening:** no one / the whole room.
- **Why here:** sound before image; the film begins in the ear.
- **Feel:** attention. **Changes:** silence becomes session.

### SHOT 2 — L1 · "This court is now in session."
- **Lens:** 50mm · **height:** Center Court floor, looking up · **size:** MCU on Judge Zero · **move:** slow push-in (the room's attention converging on him).
- **Speaking:** Judge Zero. **Listening:** both tables — and us.
- **Why this angle:** first face of the film must be the authority, framed under the seal so brand and character fuse in one image.
- **Performance:** eyes track left across the room *before* the line; the blink comes after the final word, never during.
- **Feel:** reverence. **Changes:** the trial exists now.

### SHOT 3 — MASTER *(no line — locked, animated, in the can)*
The crane reveal. Geography lesson. Warm left, grey right, empty glowing centre.

### SHOT 4 — L2 · "Good. I have questions."
- **Lens:** 35mm · **height:** eye level · **size:** medium, tracking · **move:** low arc following him out from the defence table (energy entering a still room).
- **Speaking:** Lawyer Zero. **Listening:** Judge Zero above; the Representatives across the aisle, soft in the background right.
- **Why this angle:** his introduction is *movement* — the camera physically can't stay still around him, which is the character.
- **Performance:** button the jacket on the line's first word; half-smile on the last; wings flick once as he clears the table.
- **Feel:** anticipation — this one's fun. **Changes:** the defence has the floor.

## ACT II — THE CROSS-EXAMINATION
*Each exhibit is three cuts: A (the ask) → B (the Silence at the grey table — never skipped, 0.5s) → C (the exhibit igniting over the floor emblem). The A-cut carries the line and is specified per shot; B is always the same card: 35mm, slightly high, five figures, gold light spilling over them, nothing igniting; C is always over Center Court.*

### SHOT 5 — L3 · "…the exact moment it broke?"
- **A-cut:** 50mm · eye level · medium on Lawyer Zero, Center Court, grey table soft in the right of frame · slow push (first question deserves weight).
- **Listening:** the five, motionless; Judge Zero above, watching over glasses.
- **Feel:** the game begins. **Changes:** first evidence exists — and one table couldn't answer.

### SHOT 6 — L4 · "…*why*? Anyone can say 'wrong.'"
- **A-cut:** 35mm · eye level · medium-wide · whip-pan FROM the grey table TO him (the camera itself asks "well?").
- **Feel:** momentum. **Changes:** the pattern of silence is established.

### SHOT 7 — L5 · "A concept gap. A calculation slip. Carelessness. Or time?"
- **A-cut:** 35mm · eye level · full shot · 180° orbit while he walks against it, one icon igniting per beat.
- **Why:** four ideas in one line — the orbit gives each its own background.
- **Performance:** he counts on his claws; cut lands on each syllable. **Feel:** virtuosity.

### SHOT 8 — L6 · "The same mistake. Eight times. Who noticed?"
- **A-cut:** 24mm · rising · medium going wide — fast pull-back as the timeline multiplies past frame edges.
- **Why:** the line is about scale; the camera *shows* scale on the exact word "weeks."
- **Feel:** quiet alarm — that's me, thinks every student watching.

### SHOT 9 — L7 · "And who turns that mistake into a plan?"
- **A-cut:** 50mm · eye level · medium · **locked** — first static A-cut of the act; the transformation (chaos → architecture) does the moving.
- **Why:** stillness marks the emotional turn from prosecution to advocacy.
- **Feel:** hope enters the film here. **Changes:** errors stop being shameful.

### SHOT 10 — L8 · "Who tells the student what comes next?"
- **A-cut:** 50mm · rising with the card · MCU · slow rise (the next step literally lifts).
- **Feel:** direction. **Changes:** the future has a shape.

### SHOT 11 — L9 · "…with them — first question to target score?"
- **A-cut:** 24mm · crane ascending · wide · the luminous path unrolls from his feet toward the bench.
- **Why:** the film's biggest camera move on the film's biggest promise.
- **Feel:** scope — a journey, not an app. **Changes:** the case is complete.

### SHOT 12 — L10 · "The defence rests."
- **Lens:** 85mm · eye level · MCU · **locked** — the first truly still frame since Shot 4.
- **Speaking:** Lawyer Zero, back to the grey table, facing the bench. **Listening:** Judge Zero — and behind Lawyer Zero, five grey figures in soft focus, perfectly still.
- **Performance:** straighten the tie once; the smile is *finished*, not performed. Music dies on the final word.
- **Feel:** held breath. **Changes:** the film's energy transfers from him to the Judge.

## ACT III — THE VERDICT
*Camera 2 axis, three escalating sizes. Locked off throughout — after thirty seconds of motion, stillness is the loudest choice available.*

### SHOT 13A — L11 · "You are intelligent."
- **Lens:** 50mm · low · MCU · locked. He closes the file, removes the glasses, sets them down — *then* the line, straight down the lens.
- **Listening:** the Representatives — who STAND, together, respectfully. Their only movement of the film, held in the frame's edge in the following B-roll insert (85mm, slightly high, the five rising in unison — 1s).
- **Feel:** fairness. The verdict is not cruelty. **Changes:** the grey table is released with dignity.

### SHOT 13B — L12 · "But this one was built for students."
- **Lens:** 85mm · low · CU · a push-in of only a few inches (a verdict crossing the room).
- **Performance:** without the glasses his eyes are fully readable for the first time in the film. Hold one full second on them before the line.
- **Feel:** the hammer, gently laid down. **Changes:** the trial is decided.

### SHOT 14A — L13 · "It does not just solve math."
- **Lens:** 24mm · Center Court · wide · the orbit begins as every exhibit re-ignites into one constellation.
- **Speaking:** Judge Zero above. **Listening:** Lawyer Zero *inside* the light, arms loose — insert his upturned face (85mm CU, 1s): the advocate becoming a student again.
- **Feel:** awe. **Changes:** features fuse into a system.

### SHOT 14B — L14 · "It understands how students learn."
- **Lens:** 35mm · orbit decelerating to rest on Judge Zero framed through the orbiting holograms · MCU.
- **Why:** the thesis line ends on the face that has earned it, seen *through* the system he's describing.
- **Feel:** conviction. **Changes:** the film's claim is stated — once, plainly.

### SHOTS 15A/B/C — L15 · L16 · L17 · The Three Laws
- Three matched cuts on the Camera-2 axis: 35mm full → 50mm MCU → 85mm CU. Identical framing logic, tightening. Each line burns into the floor as spoken; the grey table visible in 15A's edges, lit by laws that are not about them.
- **Performance:** identical cadence on 15A/15B; 15C a half-step softer.
- **Feel:** scripture. **Changes:** the argument becomes law.

## ACT IV — THE MERGE

### SHOT 16 — L18 · "Students don't need another chatbot."
- **Lens:** 35mm · crane descending WITH him · full shot, low · the one broken rule of the film: he leaves the bench.
- **Speaking:** Judge Zero, mid-descent, cane-first weight on every step. **Listening:** Lawyer Zero waiting on the floor emblem; the room already dissolving; the Representatives fading out — *without defeat* — during this shot.
- **Feel:** something sacred is ending so something better can start. **Changes:** the geography that ruled the whole film is dissolved by the one who ruled it.

### SHOT 17 — L19 · "They need a mentor."
- **Lens:** 50mm · eye level · two-shot arriving at pure profile via a slow 90° dolly · shallow depth, room gone to black beyond two shafts of light.
- **Speaking:** Lawyer Zero — quiet, close. **Listening:** Judge Zero, and the audience.
- **Performance — the film lives here:** they stop. A beat of nothing. Then both smile *at the same moment* — warm, unhurried, between two people who have known each other forever. The smile gets its own animation pass and its own review.
- **Feel:** recognition — "they're the same." **Changes:** the audience solves the film one second before it confirms.

### SHOT 18 — The Merge *(no line — silence is the design)*
- **Lens:** 50mm → slow push through the light · the blue energy rises from the floor emblem, robe and suit dissolve, two silhouettes become one Zero. He smiles once. Black.
- **Why no dialogue:** the image is the thesis; a line here would be the film flinching.
- **Feel:** wonder, then warmth. **Changes:** two characters become one product truth.

### SHOT 19 — Title card. Gold on black. One final soft gavel tap.

# PART 3 — PERFORMANCE DIRECTION (direct actors, not assets)

**JUDGE ZERO** — *stillness with mass.* 0.7× tempo, always. He never gestures on a line; the words do the lifting. Blinks are events: one after L1, one before L12, none during the Three Laws. Breath visible only twice — before L11 and at the top of the descent. The glasses removal in 13A is the performance of the whole act: unhurried, two hands, set down without sound. On the steps: cane, weight, foot — never foot first. His smile exists exactly once, in Shot 17, and it should look like it has been waiting there for years.

**LAWYER ZERO** — *momentum with warmth.* 1.2× tempo. Gestures land a half-beat BEFORE the word. He plays every question to the grey table but throws the punchline to the camera. Wings flick on emphasis, tail counterweights every turn — the body is always slightly ahead of the feet. The shift: from L7 onward the showman recedes and the believer shows through; by L10 he is still; L19 is the only line delivered soft, and it must sound like it costs him nothing to hand the film back to the old man. Never smug. He is delighted, not superior.

**THE GENERAL AI REPRESENTATIVES** — *presence without personhood.* They breathe in unison, barely. Reactions are singular and small: ONE head turning toward Exhibit A. A collective micro-shift at the L5 icon storm. Absolute stillness through L10–L12 — and then their one act: standing, together, when the verdict lands. They rise like a curtain, not like the condemned. They are never comic, never ashamed, never menacing. They simply were not built for this — and the film treats that as fact, not failure.

**THE COURTROOM** — the fourth performer, always running: particles rising, energy veins flowing, seal breathing at a 4-second cycle, brighter on each exhibit. At L10's silence, all ambient motion drops to half speed — the room holds its breath with the audience. During Shot 18 every particle in the world reverses and drifts *toward* the merge.

# PART 4 — THE PRODUCTION PIPELINE (researched, strongest available)

Catalog-verified against Higgsfield's current model set. This is the quality-first path, not the convenient one.

## Stage 0 — Voice, before anything
All 19 lines recorded first. Two routes:
- **Preferred:** human VO (one actor, both roles, single session, alternating lines). Upload via the widget; each line becomes an `audio` media.
- **Fallback:** `generate_audio` / Seed Audio TTS with a cloned or reference voice (`create_voice` from a sample of the chosen actor) — per line, so pacing can be directed line by line.
**Picture is cut to the read. Never the reverse.**

## Stage 1 — Key frames (per shot)
`nano_banana_pro`, every shot generated **from the locked Master Frame + character masters as references** — never from text alone. One approved still per shot card. This is the storyboard-to-layout step, and it's where composition, blocking and continuity are checked BEFORE any motion money is spent.

## Stage 2 — Motion
- **Non-dialogue shots** (1, 3 ✅, 8–11 C-cuts, 14 orbit, 16, 18): `kling3_0`, **mode `pro`** — std was for tests; production shots render pro. Motion-only prompts from the approved still; one camera move; 5s generated, 3–4s used.
- **Dialogue shots** (every L-line): **`seedance_2_0`, mode `std`, 1080p** — start_image = the approved key frame, **audio_references = the recorded line**, so the mouth, breath and micro-timing are *driven by the actual read* rather than guessed. Genre hint `drama`. This is the researched lip-sync path; `wan2_7` (synchronized-audio, character-consistent) is the A/B alternative if a given shot fights Seedance.
- **Performance transfer (escalation):** if a dialogue shot needs acting the models can't invent — Shot 17's smile above all — `motion_control` can puppeteer the character from a human reference performance recorded on a phone. Shoot the beat yourself, transfer it. This is the highest-fidelity tool we have for the film's most important two seconds.

## Stage 3 — Finish
- `upscale_video` every kept shot to 4K.
- Conform in an editor: cut to VO, the 0:38-silence protected, score and SFX per the audio design, −14 LUFS, burned subtitles, safe zones.

## The honest line (unchanged, still true)
Seedance's audio-driven animation and Kling pro raise the ceiling a lot — but **Shot 17 remains flagged for manual animation or motion-transfer from a human performance.** A simultaneous two-character emotional smile is the single hardest thing in animation; we do not gamble the film's heart on a slot machine. Everything else in this pipeline is proven or provable shot by shot.

## Production order (unchanged discipline)
Master Frame ✅ → the three re-used camera stills (Cam 2 · Cam 12/A · the B reverse) → per-shot key frames in script order → VO record → dialogue shots (Seedance) → ambient/motion shots (Kling pro) → Act IV last, when the room is second nature → upscale → conform.

*One shot at a time. Approved before the next. That's how features are made.*
