## The 180° line
**The camera never crosses the centre aisle.** Judge Zero is always up-frame. Lawyer Zero is always camera-LEFT. The General AI Representatives are always camera-RIGHT. If a shot puts Lawyer Zero on the right, it is a continuity error — reject it.

## Height hierarchy (says everything without a line of dialogue)
1. **The emblem** — highest, above everything
2. **Judge Zero** — on the bench, always shot from below
3. **Lawyer Zero** — standing on the floor, eye level
4. **The General AI Representatives** — seated, always shot slightly from above

# 2. THE THIRD PARTY — *the shot I kept forgetting*

## THE GENERAL AI REPRESENTATIVES

| | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Count** | Five. Identical. Always five |
| **Position** | Seated at the opposing table, camera-right. **They never move from it** until the room dissolves in Act IV |
| **Design** | Humanoid, abstract, smooth featureless heads, no faces, no eyes, a single faint horizontal light line. Plain grey formal attire. Cool desaturated grey-blue, drained of warmth. **They emit no light of their own** |
| **Read** | Neutral, passive, silent, patient. **Not villains.** Competent but empty. The contrast against a warm expressive dragon *is* the argument |
| **Dialogue** | None. Ever. They never speak, never confess, never object |
| **Reaction** | Minimal and natural — a head turning slowly toward an exhibit, a slight shift when the gavel falls. Never shock, never shame, never comedy |

### Why they must be in nearly every frame
Without them there is no trial — just a dragon listing features. **Their presence is what converts a feature list into a story.** Every wide and every over-the-shoulder must contain them, even softly out of focus.

### The visual argument, stated once
When an exhibit ignites over Center Court, gold light spills across **both** tables. Lawyer Zero's side takes the light and glows. The grey side is lit by it but reflects nothing back — **no hologram of their own ever appears above them.** That is the whole case, made in light. Nobody has to say it.

**Legal:** generic, unnamed, unbranded. Never shown wrong, failing, or admitting anything. Silent because these questions fall outside what they were built for — never because they are inferior.

# 3. THE SHOT CARD — *required before any generation*

Every shot in this film is specified with all six fields. **No card, no generation.**

```
SHOT ##
CAMERA:      position, height, lens, and the ONE move
JUDGE ZERO:  where, doing what
LAWYER ZERO: where, doing what
GENERAL AI:  where, doing what          ← if this says "not in frame", justify it
PURPOSE:     why this shot exists at all
COMMUNICATES: what the audience learns that they didn't know one second ago
```

**A shot that can't fill in all six is cut, not generated.**

# 4. THE FILM, SHOT BY SHOT

## ACT I — THE COURT (0:00 – 0:10)

### SHOT 1 — *The Strike* · 0:00–0:03
- **CAMERA:** Macro, 85mm, locked. On the gavel at the bench.
- **JUDGE ZERO:** At the bench. Only his hand and the gavel in frame.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Off frame (at the defence table).
- **GENERAL AI:** Off frame. *Justified — this is a macro insert, and the next shot pays the room off immediately.*
- **PURPOSE:** Sound-first hook inside 3 seconds.
- **COMMUNICATES:** We are in a court. Something is beginning.

### SHOT 2 — *The Bench* · 0:03–0:07
- **CAMERA:** Low angle from Center Court looking UP at the bench. Slow push-in. 35mm.
- **JUDGE ZERO:** Seated at the bench, the emblem glowing above and behind him. Speaks.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Not in frame — he is below and camera-left of this angle.
- **GENERAL AI:** Not in frame. *Justified — establishing the authority first; Shot 3 reveals both tables.*
- **PURPOSE:** Establish who controls this room.
- **COMMUNICATES:** Judge Zero + the emblem = the authority of this court.
- **[JZ]:** "This court is now in session."

### SHOT 3 — *The Full Court* · 0:07–0:09 · **THE MASTER SHOT**
- **CAMERA:** Wide, high, from the gallery. One slow crane down and forward. 24mm. **This is the shot that teaches the audience the geography — every later angle depends on it.**
- **JUDGE ZERO:** Elevated at the bench, up-frame centre.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Standing at the defence table, camera-LEFT, beside the warm gold Si Math AI core.
- **GENERAL AI:** All five seated at the opposing table, camera-RIGHT, cool grey, motionless.
- **PURPOSE:** Lock the geography. Establish that there are two opposing sides.
- **COMMUNICATES:** This is a trial. Warm side vs. grey side. Everything after this is read against this frame.
- **VO:** None. Music and room tone.

### SHOT 4 — *He Rises* · 0:09–0:10
- **CAMERA:** Low tracking arc, camera-left, following Lawyer Zero as he stands at the defence table.
- **JUDGE ZERO:** Visible up-frame behind him, at the bench, watching.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Stands, buttons his jacket, steps out from the defence table.
- **GENERAL AI:** Soft in the background across the aisle, camera-right, unmoving.
- **PURPOSE:** Introduce the advocate and his energy against the Judge's stillness.
- **COMMUNICATES:** This one is going to fight for his side.
- **[LZ]:** "Good. I have questions."

## ACT II — THE CROSS-EXAMINATION (0:10 – 0:38)

> **The repeating grammar. Every exhibit uses these three beats — and beat B is the one that carries the argument.**
>
> **A — THE ASK.** Lawyer Zero in Center Court, camera-left of frame, addressing the grey table. Judge Zero visible above and behind. *He asks the question.*
> **B — THE SILENCE.** Cut to the opposing table, camera-right. Five grey figures. **No hologram above them.** Gold light from the exhibit spills across them and they reflect nothing. Hold 0.5s. *This beat is the whole film. Never skip it.*
> **C — THE EXHIBIT.** The hologram ignites over Center Court, above the floor emblem. The room's energy veins surge. The seal pulses. Lawyer Zero is lit gold by it.

### SHOT 5 — **EXHIBIT A: The First Mistake** · 0:10–0:15
- **CAMERA:** A — medium on Lawyer Zero, Center Court, Judge Zero soft above. B — whip to the grey table. C — push in on the exhibit.
- **JUDGE ZERO:** At the bench, watching, motionless.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Center Court, circling the exhibit, watching the grey table not the hologram.
- **GENERAL AI:** Seated, camera-right. One head turns slowly toward the exhibit. Nothing above them.
- **PURPOSE:** First evidence. Establish the three-beat grammar.
- **COMMUNICATES:** One side can answer this. The other cannot.
- **[LZ]:** "Who saw the exact moment it broke?"
- **Exhibit:** `Step 1 ✓ / Step 2 ✓ / Step 3 ✗` — Step 3 ignites crimson.

### SHOTS 6–11 — **EXHIBITS B–G** · 0:15–0:36
Same three-beat grammar, accelerating. Blocking constant: **Judge above, Lawyer camera-left in Center Court, grey table camera-right.** What changes is only the exhibit and the cutting rhythm.

| Shot | Exhibit | Line | Camera variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | `ROOT CAUSE` | "And who knows *why*? Anyone can say 'wrong.'" | Whip-pan to grey table and back |
| 7 | 4 icons: concept / calculation / careless / time | "A concept gap. A calculation slip. Carelessness. Or time?" | 180° orbit of Center Court; Lawyer walks against it |
| 8 | `PATTERN — 8× / 6 WEEKS` | "The same mistake. Eight times. Who noticed?" | Fast pull-back; timeline exceeds frame |
| 9 | `FOCUS PRACTICE` | "And who turns that mistake into a plan?" | Locked off — let the transformation carry it |
| 10 | `NEXT LESSON` | "Who tells the student what comes next?" | Slow rise with the card |
| 11 | `JOURNEY MAP` | "Who walks the whole road — first question to target score?" | Crane up; the path runs the length of the floor |

**Continuity note for Shot 11:** the journey path begins at Lawyer Zero's feet in Center Court and runs *toward the bench*, not off into abstract space. It stays inside the room.

### SHOT 12 — *The Defence Rests* · 0:36–0:38
- **CAMERA:** Static mid-shot, Center Court. **First time the camera stops moving since Shot 4.**
- **JUDGE ZERO:** At the bench, up-frame, in focus for the first time in the act.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Turns his back on the grey table, faces the bench, straightens his tie.
- **GENERAL AI:** Behind him, camera-right, soft focus, still silent.
- **PURPOSE:** End the argument. Hand the film to the Judge.
- **COMMUNICATES:** The case is finished. Now we wait for a ruling.
- **[LZ]:** "The defence rests."
- **Music cuts to silence on the last word.**

## ACT IV — THE MERGE (0:57 – 1:12)

> The only sequence where the blocking is allowed to break — **and it breaks on purpose.** Judge Zero leaves the bench for the first and only time. That's why it lands.

### SHOT 16 — *He Steps Down* · 0:57–1:02
- **CAMERA:** Crane down from the bench to floor level, matching his descent.
- **JUDGE ZERO:** Descends the five marble steps. Cane first, weight, step. Never hurries.
- **LAWYER ZERO:** Waiting in Center Court, watching him come.
- **GENERAL AI:** The room is already dissolving around them; the grey table fades out during this shot. **This is where they exit the film** — quietly, without defeat.
- **PURPOSE:** Break the geography that has held for a whole film.
- **COMMUNICATES:** Something has changed. The trial is over; something else is starting.
- **[JZ]:** "Students don't need another chatbot."

### SHOT 17 — *Face to Face* · 1:02–1:07
- **CAMERA:** Slow 90° dolly around them, ending profile two-shot, eye level.
- **JUDGE ZERO / LAWYER ZERO:** Center Court, on the floor emblem, face to face. They stop. A beat. **Both smile.**
- **GENERAL AI:** Gone. The room is black beyond the two shafts of light.
- **PURPOSE:** The emotional peak.
- **COMMUNICATES:** These two belong to each other.
- **[LZ]:** "They need a mentor."
- **⚠ Highest-risk shot in the film.** Own animation pass, own review.

### SHOT 18 — *One Zero* · 1:07–1:12
- **CAMERA:** Slow push-in through the merge; hold on the single figure.
- **BOTH:** Blue energy rises from the floor emblem between them. Robe and suit dissolve into light. They become **one Zero**, standing alone. He smiles once. Black.
- **PURPOSE:** The thesis as an image.
- **COMMUNICATES:** The judge and the mentor are one system.
- **VO:** None.
- **Two candle flames becoming one.** Never a teleport, never an explosion, no white flash.

### SHOT 19 — *Logo* · 1:12–1:16
Black. Gold logo. `SI MATH AI / More than an AI. A complete learning journey. / simathAI.com`

# 5. GENERATION ORDER — *build the room, then shoot in it*

Generating shots in story order is wrong. **Generate in dependency order.**

| Step | What | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | General AI Representatives design | Third party missing from every existing frame |
| **2** | **SHOT 3 — the master wide, all three parties in place** | Every other angle is derived from this one frame |
| **3** | Shot 2 (bench), Shot 12 (Center Court), grey-table reverse | The three re-used camera positions of the whole film |
| **4** | Exhibit inserts | Slot into the positions above |
| **5** | Act IV | Only after the geography is proven |

**Rule:** every character shot is generated using the master wide as an environment reference, so the room, light direction and lens language stay identical across cuts. A shot generated from a text description instead of the master **will** drift, and drift reads as a different room.
