## WHAT CHANGES STRUCTURALLY

**v2:** Lawyer Zero interrogates the silent Representatives. The Judge watches, then rules.

**v3:** **The Court interrogates.** Judge Zero puts each question to the Representatives from the
bench — calm, institutional, inescapable. They cannot answer. **Into each silence steps Lawyer
Zero** — not narrating, *doing*: he answers on his client's behalf with evidence that ignites over
Center Court. The film stops being a monologue with visual aids and becomes an actual exchange with
three forces: the authority who asks, the silence that condemns, and the advocate who proves.

Why this raises the drama (the screening notes, answered structurally):
- **Every question now has two possible answerers** — and one side never answers. Seven times.
- The Representatives' silence stops being ambience and becomes **testimony**.
- Lawyer Zero's every appearance is an *entrance through someone else's silence* — the room hands
  him the floor seven times, and he owns it (walk, turn, present, ignite).
- The Judge is on screen, speaking, in command, for the whole film — with real lip sync.

The verdict lands differently too: the interrogator himself rules. He asked; he watched them fail
to answer; he watched the young one answer everything. **"You are intelligent. But this one was
built for students"** is now the conclusion of *his own* examination.
