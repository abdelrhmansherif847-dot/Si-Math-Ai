# SI MATH AI — "THE AI COURT"
# SCRIPT v4 — "THE FAILED ANSWER" · ⏳ PROPOSAL, AWAITING PER-LINE OWNER APPROVAL

Builds on v3 (approved in structure) with the owner's third-screening notes: **a hook in the first
three seconds · the Representatives try to answer and fail · Lawyer Zero dominates · the room never
freezes · unforgettable lines · the ending locked exactly as specified.**

**The one design change this requires:** the Representatives speak — twice, briefly, early. This
amends `03`'s "silent" canon by owner directive. It does not make them villains or comedians: their
attempts are sincere, competent, and **wrong-register** — they answer an easier question than the
one they were asked. That is the whole tragedy, and it is why the silence afterwards is devastating.

**Their voice is the one place synthetic is correct:** clean, pleasant, capable, lightly processed —
the confident tone of a chatbot. The only TTS voice in the finished film belongs to the synthetic
characters. That is diegetic, not a shortcut — and it means the human VO session stays two roles.

## THE ROOM NEVER FREEZES — listening coverage (note 4)

Written into every take, per the `05` performance layer, now with the failure beats woven in:

- **During v4-02** (the Rep answers): Lawyer Zero's head turns sharply toward the grey table — the
  only time in the film *he* is surprised. The Judge does not move at all.
- **During every JZ question:** the Reps' light-lines flicker attention toward the bench; LZ listens
  at his table like a coiled spring — until his entrances.
- **During every LZ answer:** the Judge watches *over the glasses*; the Reps track the evidence,
  never him — they understand exhibits, not advocates.
- Breath, blinks, weight shifts continuous per Law 16. A frozen listener is a failed frame.
