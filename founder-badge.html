◆ Rationale

Why the Founder Badge exists

Founding members back a platform before it is obvious that it works. The Founder Badge is how that is recognised — permanently, rather than with a temporary discount that quietly expires.

Every platform's earliest members take a risk that later members do not. They commit while the product is still proving itself, they use features before those features are polished, and their feedback shapes what the platform becomes. In the case of Si Math AI, early members are studying for examinations that will not wait — they are trusting a young platform with something that genuinely matters to their future.

Most companies acknowledge that with a launch discount for three months. We think that gets the logic backwards: the value a founding member provides is permanent, so the recognition should be permanent too. A Founder member's rate is locked for as long as they stay with us — not until the end of a promotional window.

The badge does three things at once:

  • It recognises early trust with a benefit that does not expire, rather than one that is withdrawn once the platform no longer needs early adopters.
  • It funds the work honestly. Founding memberships support continued development — the educator time that authors and reviews content, and the engineering that delivers it — without advertising, data resale, or diluting the free tier that keeps the platform accessible.
  • It creates a small group worth listening to. A limited founding cohort is a set of students with a long-term stake in the platform, whose feedback is worth building around.

◆ Lifetime pricing

How the lifetime price lock works

The rule in one sentence: Founder members pay 50% of the price, and that discounted price is locked forever as long as the membership remains active.

SituationWhat happens to a Founder member
Si Math AI raises its pricesNothing changes. The Founder rate is locked; increases do not apply.
New features shipIncluded. Founder membership covers the complete platform as it develops.
The Founder memberships sell outExisting Founder members are unaffected. Their rate and badge continue exactly as before.
The membership renewsIt renews at the locked Founder rate, not at the current public price.
The membership lapses or is cancelledThe lock is released. The Founder rate and the reserved place return to the pool and cannot be recovered once the remaining memberships are gone.

The condition is the important part and we state it plainly rather than burying it: the lock lasts as long as the membership remains active. It is attached to a continuing membership, not to an account that has lapsed. That is the only condition, and there are no others.

◆ Scarcity

Why only a limited number exist

👑 Only 3 Founder memberships remain

The cap is not a marketing device. It is what makes a permanent price lock possible to honour.

A lifetime 50% discount is a commitment against every future price the platform will ever charge. Unlike a launch promotion, it never ends and cannot be quietly revised. That commitment is straightforward to honour for a small founding group. Extended to an unlimited number of members, it becomes a liability that grows with the platform — and companies that make that mistake resolve it in one of two ways: they break the promise, or they compensate by raising prices for everyone else and thinning out the free tier.

We would rather not be in the position of doing either. So:

  • The number is fixed. It is not reissued when the current memberships are claimed, and there is no second founding round under a different name.
  • It is genuinely nearly gone. Only 3 memberships remain at the time of writing. When they are claimed, Founder membership closes.
  • Closing it protects everyone else. A bounded founding cohort is how the free tier stays genuinely useful and the standard price stays reasonable for students who arrive later.
  • Existing Founders are never affected. Whatever the platform charges in future, a Founder's locked rate continues unchanged.

On urgency: the scarcity here is real and structural, but a Founder membership is worth claiming only if the platform is right for you. Si Math AI has a free tier for exactly that reason — start there, use it properly, and upgrade only if it earns it.

Common questions

What is the Si Math AI Founder Badge?

The Founder Badge is a founding membership of Si Math AI. Founder members receive a 50% lifetime discount that is locked forever for as long as the membership remains active, a permanent Founder badge on their profile, and access to the complete platform. The number of Founder memberships is strictly limited and only 3 remain.

Is the Founder discount permanent?

Yes. The 50% discount is a lifetime discount, and the discounted price is locked forever for as long as the membership remains active. Future price increases do not apply to a Founder member. If the membership lapses, the Founder rate and the reserved place are released and cannot be recovered once the remaining memberships are gone.

How many Founder Badge memberships are left?

Only 3 Founder Badge memberships remain. The number is fixed and is not reissued when the current memberships are claimed.

Why is the number of Founder memberships limited?

A permanent 50% price lock is a lasting commitment against every future price of the platform. Si Math AI can honour that commitment for a small founding group without compromising the free tier or the pace of development, but not for an unlimited number of members. The cap is what makes the lifetime lock sustainable rather than a promise that has to be withdrawn later.

What happens to my Founder price if Si Math AI raises its prices?

Nothing. Future price increases are locked out for Founder members. The rate agreed at the time of joining remains in force for as long as the membership stays active, regardless of what the platform charges new members later.

What does the Founder Badge actually look like?

It is a permanent badge displayed on your Si Math AI profile, marking the account as a founding membership. It does not expire while the membership remains active.

Yes, and we would recommend it. Si Math AI has a permanent free tier that needs no credit card. Use it, ask Zero real questions from your own prep book, look at what the Weakness Analyzer finds, and decide from there. Start free.
