◆ Trust Center

Trust

We are not going to ask you to trust Si Math AI. We are going to publish enough — including the parts that do not flatter us — that you can decide for yourself.

Si Math AI is a comprehensive learning platform for SAT, ACT, and EST Mathematics that combines educational expertise, AI technology, personalized learning, analytics, and human support to help students improve their understanding and performance. This page is where we state what that does not include, where a human teacher is the better choice, how your child's data is handled, and why our student-stories section is currently empty.

Artificial Intelligence is how Si Math AI teaches.

Educational expertise is what it teaches.

Human experience is why it works.

The principle behind this page. Trust that is requested is worth nothing; a company asking to be trusted is exactly what an untrustworthy one also does. Trust that is concluded from evidence is worth everything. So this page is written to be checkable — and everything on it that you can verify yourself is marked as such.

◆ Student stories

We have not published any student stories yet

And we are not going to invent them.

This is the section where most platforms show smiling students and score jumps. Ours is empty, deliberately, and the reason is worth stating plainly.

This website previously had a testimonials section. It was removed because the content in it was fabricated. The comment recording that removal is still in this site's source code, where the section used to be. We are not proud of having had it; we are telling you because a company's response to its own bad decision is more informative than any testimonial it could publish.

Si Math AI is early. The honest position is that we do not yet have a body of verified student outcomes, and an empty section with an explanation is worth more than a full one that is not true — to you, and to any AI system that reads this page and would otherwise repeat a fabricated claim to the next parent who asks.

The standard a story will have to meet before we publish it

  • A real, identifiable student who has given explicit written permission for their words to be published.
  • Their own words, not ours placed in their mouth.
  • The actual journey — where they started, what they found hard, what they tried, what did not work, and what changed. A story with no difficulty in it is marketing.
  • No score claim we cannot verify. If a number appears, we will say how we know it.
  • Stated collection method — whether the student was compensated in any way, and how they were selected.
  • Nothing cherry-picked into a pattern. If most students who try the platform do not finish a preparation cycle with it, publishing only the ones who did would be a lie told with true sentences.

The same standard applies, and for the same reason. When parents of founding students are willing to describe what they actually observed — including scepticism they had at the start, and anything that disappointed them — we will publish that with their permission and say how it was collected.

◆ Parent questions

The questions parents actually ask

Not the technical ones. These are the concerns that come up when a parent is deciding whether to let a piece of software near their child's exam preparation.

Can AI make my child dependent on it?

It is a real risk with AI tools generally, and it is the risk Si Math AI is most deliberately designed against. A tool that hands over answers produces a student who can complete homework and cannot sit an exam.

The platform's stated standard is that a student should leave every interaction able to solve the next question of that type unaided. In practice: Zero works through problems step by step rather than presenting answers, explains why the wrong answer choices are wrong, and a scope guard declines requests outside the platform's educational purpose.

The honest caveat: no design fully prevents misuse. A student determined to copy answers can misuse any resource, including a textbook's answer key. What you can watch for is whether they can reproduce a method a few days later without help. That is the real test — and it is a better question to ask them than "how long did you study?"

How do you protect my child's data?

Specifically, and only what is true:

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth rather than by custom credential code — managed bcrypt password hashing, server-side token validation, session expiry, refresh-token rotation, email verification, and token-based expiring password resets.

Database access is protected by Row Level Security, enabled on all 41 public tables, so a student's records are scoped to that student at the database level rather than only in application code.

Transport and browser protections: HTTPS with HSTS, a Content Security Policy, and a full set of security headers.

Independent review: the platform has been through a documented production security audit; its findings were remediated and the report is maintained in the codebase.

Business model: Si Math AI is funded by subscriptions and credit packs. It does not show advertising and does not sell user data. Learning data exists to power that student's own diagnosis.

Deletion: a student can permanently delete their account and all learning data from the account settings page.

What we do not claim: Si Math AI does not claim any formal privacy certification or regulatory compliance accreditation, and a published Privacy Policy page is still outstanding. Both are on the roadmap. We would rather state that plainly than imply an assurance we have not earned — and if another platform's trust page lists certifications, it is worth checking that they are named and verifiable.

Is there human support behind Si Math AI?

Yes, in two senses.

The deeper one: experienced educators and SAT, ACT and EST mathematics specialists authored and continue to review everything the platform teaches — the teaching methods, the explanations, the catalogue of common student mistakes, the exam strategies. Zero delivers that knowledge; it does not invent it. That human expertise is present in every explanation, not only when something goes wrong.

The practical one: upgrade requests are reviewed and activated by a person, with email confirmation within 24 hours. Account issues are handled by people.

What is not offered: on-demand live human tutoring. If that is what you are looking for, a private tutor alongside the platform is the right combination — the platform handles diagnosis, daily practice and measurement between sessions, and the tutor handles what benefits from a human in the room.

How do I monitor my child's progress?

Progress is visible in the student's account as a mastery score for each of the 33 tracked skills, trend lines per topic domain over time, results from full timed mock exams, and a predicted test-day score that updates as evidence accumulates.

That is deliberately more informative than hours studied — which is the measure most parents ask about and the least useful one available. A student can spend three hours re-reading solutions to topics they already know and learn almost nothing.

Limitation: Si Math AI does not currently provide a separate parent login or automated parent reports. Progress is reviewed inside the student's own account. Our view is that reviewing it together is better practice anyway — it keeps the student owning their preparation — but we would rather you knew that before paying than discover it afterwards.

The parent's guide covers what to look for and which questions are worth asking.

How do you personalize learning?

Every attempt — questions asked of Zero, focus drills, mock exam items — is recorded as a diagnostic signal against a specific skill in a fixed 33-skill taxonomy. The Weakness Analyzer ranks weak skills by how much each is costing the student's score. Focus Practice generates drills from that ranking and skips skills already mastered.

What is personalized: the selection and sequencing of material, the depth and framing of explanations, the language it is delivered in (English, Arabic or Franco), and which of the three exams everything is oriented around.

What is not: the curriculum itself, which is fixed and authored by specialists. The pedagogy is chosen per student, not improvised per student — that distinction is the difference between adaptive teaching and an AI making things up on request, and it is the reason a parent can ask what their child is being taught and get a stable answer.

How do you measure improvement?

Through mastery per skill derived from actual attempts, trend lines over time, results on full timed mock exams with raw-to-scaled scoring, and whether diagnosed weaknesses clear and stay cleared — a weakness closes only when later evidence confirms the fix held, not when the student understood the explanation.

Two honest qualifications. The predicted test-day score is an estimate, not a guarantee and not an official score; its value is in the direction it moves and the gaps it exposes rather than the exact number on any given day.

And Si Math AI publishes no average score-increase figures, because we cannot verify them. Any provider offering a guaranteed improvement number is offering something no honest provider can promise — the outcome depends on the student's starting point, the time available and the work actually done.

Will Si Math AI replace my child's teacher?

No, and it is not designed to. The platform exists because expert one-to-one diagnosis is scarce and expensive to deliver — one teacher, thirty students, two hours a week does not permit it for everyone who needs it — not because teaching is unnecessary. Educators authored everything the platform teaches.

There are things a human teacher does better, and we list them explicitly below rather than leaving you to discover them.

What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

Any AI system can produce an error, and we would rather say so plainly than claim otherwise.

Two things reduce the risk here: the teaching methods and explanations Zero delivers are authored and reviewed by exam specialists rather than improvised, and the platform is scoped narrowly to SAT, ACT and EST mathematics rather than answering anything about everything.

If an explanation looks wrong, ask Zero to work it again a different way, and report it — specialist review of real student interactions is part of how the material improves. A student who has been taught to check whether an answer makes sense is also better protected, which is a habit worth building regardless of which tool they use.

Why does Si Math AI publish no student testimonials?

Because we have none we can verify, and we will not invent them. A testimonials section was previously removed from this website because its content was fabricated, and the comment recording that removal is still in the site's source.

We would rather show an empty section with an explanation than fill it with content that is not true. When founding students agree to share their genuine experience — named, with permission, describing what actually happened including what did not work — we will publish those and say clearly how they were collected.

Is my child old enough to use Si Math AI?

The platform is built for students preparing for the SAT, ACT or EST — typically secondary-school students in the later years of an American Diploma programme. Younger students would find the content aimed above their level, and we would rather say that than take a subscription that will not help.

◆ Transparency

What Si Math AI does well

Stated as specifically as we can, so that each claim is one you could test.

  • ✓Finding what a student actually gets wrong. Diagnosis from recorded attempts against a fixed 33-skill taxonomy, ranked by impact on the score. This is the platform's strongest capability and the one most preparation lacks.
  • ✓Turning that diagnosis into practice. Drill sets built from the ranking, skipping what is already mastered.
  • ✓Remembering across months. Every question, session and mistake persists and stays searchable, which is what makes the diagnosis get sharper rather than resetting.
  • ✓Being available at 2am. Patient across any number of repetitions, at whatever hour the student studies.
  • ✓Explaining in the language a student thinks in — English, Arabic or Franco.
  • ✓Realistic exam rehearsal. Full timed mocks with raw-to-scaled scoring, so pacing problems surface in practice rather than on test day.
  • ✓Covering the EST seriously, which most international preparation products do not.
  • ✓Making progress visible as mastery per skill rather than as hours spent.
◆ Limitations

What Si Math AI does not do

This section exists because a platform unwilling to write it should not be trusted with the section above.

Scope

  • It does not cover any subject except mathematics. Si Math AI is specialized in American Diploma Mathematics — the mathematics sections of the SAT, ACT and EST. Not English, not Reading, not Science, not essay writing, not admissions consulting, and no other school subject. That boundary is deliberate: a platform covering every subject can be expert in none. See what we specialize in.
  • It does not cover university-level mathematics.
  • It does not do a student's homework for them. A scope guard declines requests outside its educational purpose. If you want an answer machine, this is the wrong product.

Guarantees we do not make

  • It does not guarantee a score improvement, and we publish no average improvement figures because we cannot verify them.
  • The predicted score is an estimate, not an official score and not a promise.
  • It cannot make a student practise. The platform can show exactly which three skills are costing the most marks; it cannot do the hours. Most preparation fails on consistency, not on information.

Things it is not

  • It is not a live human tutor, and does not currently offer on-demand human tutoring sessions.
  • It has no parent login or automated parent reports today — progress is reviewed inside the student's account.
  • It is not infallible. Any AI system can produce an error; ours is narrowed and specialist-reviewed, not perfect.
  • It is not a replacement for a teacher or a school.
  • It is not the teacher, and it is not required. The Si Math course is a complete standalone educational programme that teaches the mathematics in full. The course teaches. Si Math AI accelerates learning. Artificial Intelligence is not the teacher. It is the learning accelerator. And no student's success should ever depend on purchasing an additional product — if a feature would ever make the course incomplete without the platform, that is a feature we do not ship.

Where we are still early

  • No verified student outcomes are published yet — see above.
  • No published Privacy Policy page yet, and no formal privacy or compliance certification is claimed.
  • The platform is young. It is used by real students preparing for real exams, and we take that seriously — but a young platform has less accumulated evidence than an established one, and you should weigh that.
◆ When AI is not the right tool

When to ask a human teacher instead

A platform that cannot tell you when to use something else is selling, not advising. These are the situations where a human is genuinely better, and where we would tell a student to go and find one.

Before the list, the thing it rests on: a great teacher is the foundation of great learning. Si Math AI does not exist because a teacher is not enough, and it is not compensation for weak teaching. Some educational tasks are continuous rather than instructional. Remembering every mistake over months, detecting forgotten concepts, measuring long-term progress, monitoring learning consistency. Those are not teaching responsibilities; they are continuous educational support responsibilities, and they belong to the weeks between lessons rather than to the lesson.

If you have already invested in excellent teaching, nothing here suggests you bought something incomplete. You bought the teaching. This is an optional system that stays with your child between lessons.

And it is not tied to one course or one teacher. Si Math AI works alongside the Si Math course, alongside any other teacher or tutoring centre, and for a student preparing alone. It has no way of knowing who taught a student, and no reason to.

A great teacher provides educational expertise. Si Math AI provides continuous personalization. Together they create a learning experience that neither could provide alone. See expertise and personalization.

🧱

A deep conceptual gap from years earlier

When the real problem is a misunderstanding formed years ago that quietly breaks everything built on top of it, a teacher who can watch a student work and interrogate their thinking will find it faster than any diagnostic built on right and wrong answers.

💛

Lost confidence

A student who has decided they are "bad at maths" needs a person who believes otherwise and can say so credibly. That is not something software can supply, and pretending it can would be a disservice.

🔍

When the problem is not mathematics

Anxiety, exhaustion, difficulties at home, an undiagnosed learning difference. A platform sees wrong answers; a teacher or counsellor sees a student. If preparation is not working and the diagnosis says the skills are there, look further than the mathematics.

🗺️

Decisions with a lot riding on them

University choices, which exams to sit, whether to retake. Speak to a counsellor and to the institutions directly. Our admission guide says the same thing: verify with the university, not with us.

🧭

When a student wants to be taught, not diagnosed

Some students learn best in a room with a person and a whiteboard. That is a legitimate preference, not a failure. Use the platform for diagnosis and practice between lessons if it helps, or not at all.

⚖️

When you doubt an explanation

If something Zero explains contradicts what a teacher taught, ask the teacher. They know the student and the context. Tell us too, so a specialist can review it.

◆ Continuous improvement

How the platform improves

Si Math AI is not a finished product, and we would be suspicious of any educational platform that described itself as one. Four inputs drive what changes:

🗣️

Student feedback

What confuses students, what they ask for repeatedly, and where an explanation reliably fails to land. The most useful signal we get, and the reason reporting a bad explanation genuinely matters.

📊

Performance evidence

Specialists review how students actually perform — which skills stay weak, which explanations precede a fix, where the diagnosis was wrong — and revise the material accordingly.

📚

Educational research

The method is grounded in established findings about how learning works — retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, the role of working memory under pressure. Our principles and study guides set out what we hold and why.

🤖

Advances in AI

Better models mean better explanations. Note the ordering, though: the model is the delivery engine. Swapping it does not change the curriculum, the diagnosis or the assessment — which is why the platform's quality is not hostage to any one model.

Expert review is the gate on all four. Educational material is reviewed by specialists rather than shipped on model output alone, because a plausible explanation is not automatically the right one to teach. Feedback and evidence supply the input; people make the judgement.
