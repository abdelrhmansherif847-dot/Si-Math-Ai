How it works
1 · A student uses your code

They open your link. Nothing is asked of them and nothing is shown to them; they are credited to you as soon as they sign in. If they open another teacher's link before they pay, they move — the most recent link wins right up until the moment they buy something.

2 · They make their first purchase

The moment a payment is approved, the credit becomes permanent. Nothing can move that student to another teacher afterwards except a deliberate, recorded correction by an administrator.

3 · Your commission is recorded

Calculated on the plan's own price from our catalogue — never on whatever figure was typed into a payment form — and frozen at the rate you were on that day.

What earns a commission

A student's first successful paid purchase, and only that. Any plan counts, and so does a credit pack.

What does not

Renewals earn nothing in this first version of the programme — the commission is for bringing a student to Si Math AI, not for their continued subscription. Payments that fail, are rejected, are refunded or are reversed earn nothing either. A student who had already bought something before using your code is never credited: the programme rewards a new student, not a transfer of an existing one.

One student, one commission

A student can only ever generate one first purchase, so a second commission for the same student is not something we decline — it is something the system cannot create.

Commission tiers

Your rate is set by how many of your referred students have made a first purchase. It rises as you bring more, and it applies to each new commission from that point — commissions already recorded keep the rate they were earned at.

Getting paid

Every commission is recorded the moment it is earned and moves through four states, all of which you can see on your workspace page:

Earned
recorded and yours
Pending
awaiting review
Paid
sent to you
Reversed
withdrawn, with a reason
Payouts are not open yet. Commissions are being recorded now so that nothing is lost, but no money moves until we have confirmed how tax and withholding apply. There is deliberately no withdraw button here: we would rather show you nothing than a button that fails.

A reversed commission stays on your record with the reason it was withdrawn. Nothing is ever quietly deleted from your earnings.

A note on what this is not
This programme changes nothing about what your students pay. They see the same plans at the same prices as everybody else, and your commission comes out of Si Math AI's side, not theirs. You are not reselling and you are not setting prices.
